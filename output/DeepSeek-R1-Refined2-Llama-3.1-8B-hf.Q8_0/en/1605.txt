In the stillness of a prison, a figure stands, surrounded by swords that pierce the sky like jagged teeth. The air is heavy with the weight of confinement, yet the figure remains steadfast, a island of calm in a sea of turmoil. The earth beneath their feet is dry and barren, a reflection of the desolation that has taken hold of their spirit. And yet, even in this state of limbo, there is a sense of resignation, a knowing that this too shall pass. The figure waits, patient and still, for the winds of change to blow, for the swords to be removed, and for the earth to yield its secrets once more.
