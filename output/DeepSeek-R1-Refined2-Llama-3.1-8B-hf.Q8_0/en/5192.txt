In the depths of your essence, a wildfire burns, a fierce and unyielding flame that fuels your passions and drives your will. This internal conflagration is both creative and destructive, burning away the old to make space for the new, like autumn leaves surrendering to the winter snow. As you stand at the crossroads, this flame guides you, urging you to acknowledge its power and channel it wisely. Reflect on the desires that set your soul ablaze, and consider how to align them with a higher purpose, that your actions may be guided by a profound inner knowing.
