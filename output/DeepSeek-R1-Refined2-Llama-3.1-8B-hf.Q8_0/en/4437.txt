In the stillness of the depths, a vessel moves forth, propelled by the unseen forces of the heart. It navigates the twists and turns of the emotional landscape, collecting the tears of sorrow and the waves of joy. Within its walls, a king sits, steadfast and unmoved, yet connected to the ebb and flow of the subconscious. He is a master of the tides, aware of the power that lies beneath the surface. As the chariot moves forward, it leaves a trail of reflection, a path illuminated by the glow of intuition. The water's edge whispers secrets to the wind, which carries them afar, reminding that even in the calm, the depths remain, waiting to be explored.
