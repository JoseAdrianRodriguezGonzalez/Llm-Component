In the stillness, a pause is necessary, a moment to withdraw from the external world's demands and recharge. The solidity of one's foundation is reflected in the tranquility of the inner landscape, where the mind and emotions have settled into a state of calm. This is not a time for action, but for reflection, allowing the depths of one's being to reveal their true nature. The physical world, with all its demands and distractions, recedes into the background, making space for a deeper connection with one's own essence. In this peacefulness, one may discover the seeds of growth, nourished by the stability of one's inner world, and the resources available, both within and without.
