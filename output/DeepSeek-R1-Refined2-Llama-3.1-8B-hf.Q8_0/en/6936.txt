In the depths of your being, a flame burns bright, a spark of divine energy that fuels your every endeavor. It is the fire that ignites your passion, that drives you to create, to act, to will into existence the world you desire. This inner fire is both a blessing and a curse, for it can consume you whole, burning away the old to make room for the new, or it can guide you wisely, leading you toward growth and transformation. As you stand at the crossroads, consider the desires that burn within you, and ask yourself if they align with the higher purposes that call to you. Let the fire of your willpower guide you, but beware its destructive tendencies, lest you lose yourself in the flames. Instead, let it purify you, revealing the depths of your soul, and guide you toward becoming the best version of yourself.
