In this sacred landscape, we find the opportunity to reconcile our inner and outer worlds, to bring our aspirations into harmony with the practical realities of our lives. The earth's steady heartbeat teaches us to approach life with a sense of wonder, to nurture all that surrounds us, and to find balance in the dance between spirit and matter.
