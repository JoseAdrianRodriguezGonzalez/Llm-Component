In the depths of the subconscious, a storm brews, upending the calm surface of the emotional ocean. The foundations of perception are shattered, revealing the hidden currents that flow beneath the waves of consciousness. As the turbulent waters rise, the seeker is confronted with the raw power of their own emotions, unfiltered and unbridled. The secrets of the heart, long hidden, are revealed in all their glory, both beautiful and terrifying. In this moment of chaos, the opportunity arises to rebirth one's relationship with their emotions, to navigate the depths with a newfound sense of wonder and trepidation. The (Tower) stands as a testament to the transformative power of emotional upheaval, inviting the brave to embark on a journey of self-discovery, where the lines between reality and illusion are blurred, and the true nature of the self is revealed.
