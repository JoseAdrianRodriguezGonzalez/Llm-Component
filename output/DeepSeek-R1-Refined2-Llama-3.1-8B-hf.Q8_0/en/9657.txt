As you stand at the crossroads, the flames of your willpower dance before you, a tempestuous mix of destruction and rebirth. Will you tend the flames, or will they consume you whole? The choice is yours, but one thing is certain: the fire will not be silenced. It will rage on, a force both beautiful and terrifying, until you learn to harness its power, to channel it into a beacon of light that guides you toward your truest self.
