In the depths of the soul, a spark glows like embers, beckoning transformation. It is the fire that forges will, that fuels the passion to create and to destroy, to burn away the old and make way for the new. This inner flame dances with the essence of drive and determination, igniting the desire to manifest one's deepest longings. As it rises, it purifies, casting away the remnants of what no longer serves, making room for rebirth and renewal. The seeker is invited to tend this fire, to acknowledge its power and channel it wisely, that personal growth may flourish like a phoenix from the ashes. In this blaze of inspiration, the individual may rediscover their true north, aligning their actions with the higher purpose that burns within.
