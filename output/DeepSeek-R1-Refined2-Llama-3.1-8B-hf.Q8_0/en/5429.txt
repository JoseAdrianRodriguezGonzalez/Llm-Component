In the depths of surrender, a spark within stirs, a flame that flickers with the essence of creation. It is the fire that forges will, that burns away the ashes of what no longer serves, making room for the new to emerge. This is the power that resides within, a divine spark that ignites the passions, driving action and change. It is the energy of transformation, a purifying force that cleanses the old to reveal the beauty of the un manifest. As you stand at the edge of letting go, remember that this fire is both destroyer and creator, burning away the obstacles to reveal the path ahead. Listen to its whisper, and allow it to guide you towards the authentic expression of your deepest desires, that you may emerge transformed, reborn from the ashes of what once was.
