In the stillness of the night, shadows dance upon the walls, a haunting reminder of the burdens we carry. The weight of the world, with all its worries and fears, presses down upon us, making it hard to breathe. We are trapped, alone in our thoughts, with no escape from the turmoil that rages within. The physical realm, once a source of comfort and security, now feels like a prison, confining us to our darkest insecurities. Resources, once abundance, now seem scarce, a meager supply to sustain us through the long, dark night. We are forced to confront the fragility of our existence, the impermanence of all we think we know. In this darkness, we must find the strength to face our fears, to nurture what's left, and to hold on to hope, no matter how slender the thread may seem.
