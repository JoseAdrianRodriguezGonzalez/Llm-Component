In the depths of the soul, a spark ignites, a flame that flickers with the passion of creation. It is the fire that burns within, driving the will to shape reality, to forge paths where none existed. This inner torch illuminates the deepest desires, casting shadows on the aspects of self that no longer serve. As it dances, it purifies, burning away the old to make room for the new, a catalyst for transformation. The seeker is invited to tend this flame, to channel its energy wisely, that it may guide them towards their highest aspirations, and ignite a passion that fuels purposeful action. In this blaze, growth and renewal are forged, as the individual emerges, reborn, with a heart ablaze and a spirit set free.
