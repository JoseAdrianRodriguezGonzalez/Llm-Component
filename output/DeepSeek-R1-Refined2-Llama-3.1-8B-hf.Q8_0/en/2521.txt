In the stillness, a lone figure stands, arms wrapped tightly around their treasure, guarding it from the whispers of desire. The physical realm is a prison, a place of confinement, where emotions are locked away, and thoughts are buried beneath the weight of responsibility. Yet, in this isolation, a sense of control is forged, a masquerade of power in the face of uncertainty.
