In the depths of the soul, a flame burns bright, a fiery spark that ignites the passion of creation. It is the divine spark that sets the heart ablaze with purpose and drive, guiding the seeker towards manifestation and willful expression. This inner fire, a potent force, can either consume and destroy, or purify and transform, depending on how it is harnessed. As the seeker navigates the landscape of their own power, they are invited to reflect on the sources of their drive and willpower, to ensure that these energies are channeled towards higher aspirations, rather than being consumed by lower desires. By acknowledging and aligning with this inner flame, the individual can embark on a journey of personal growth, where transformation and rebirth become possible, and the old makes way for the new, like autumn leaves surrendering to the winter's chill.
