The wisdom of the heart beats in time with the pulse of the universe, offering glimpses of truth to those who dare to listen. As the hermit's light illuminates the waters, shadows are revealed, and the seeker is invited to confront the secrets they have kept hidden, to navigate the depths of their own emotional landscape, and to emerge transformed, with a deeper understanding of themselves and the world around them.
