In the depths of your soul, a spark burns Bright and unrelenting, a flame that yearns To be set free, to dance upon the wind And ignite the world with its fierce design. It is the fire that forges, that shapes and molds, Transmuting lead into gold, transforming The old into the new, as ashes rise To surrender to the blaze's pure might. This inner fire, a torch that lights the way, Guides you through the darkness, leading You to confront the shadows that Would seek to snuff the flame out of you. It is the catalyst for change, the driver Of your deepest desires, the whisper That urges you to rise above the noise And claim your power, to make your move, To seize the day and let your spirit Soar on wings of courage, unafraid To face the unknown, to walk the path Less traveled, where your true self awaits.
