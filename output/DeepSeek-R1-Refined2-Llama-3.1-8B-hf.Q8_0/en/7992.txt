In the heart of creation, a spark is ignited, kindling the flames of passion and will. Her presence is a reminder that transformation is not just a possibility, but a necessity, for it is in the fire's purifying touch that we find the courage to let go of what no longer serves us. Her wisdom whispers that the power to create and destroy is not a privilege, but a responsibility, and that the choice to wield it wisely is ours alone.
