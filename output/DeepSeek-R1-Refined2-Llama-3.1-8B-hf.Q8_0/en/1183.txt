In the stillness of dawn, where shadows dance upon the earth, a memory stirs, a longing for what's been left behind. The gentle rustle of leaves, the soft caress of soil beneath fingers, evoke a sense of nostalgia, a yearning for simplicity. The cycles of life, death, and rebirth, play out in the rhythm of nature, reminding us that all things must pass, yet from decay, new life bursts forth. In this quiet moment, we're invited to reflect on the treasures we've accumulated, the wealth of experiences, and the resources we've been given. Let us tend to the garden of our souls, nurturing what's been sown, and pruning what no longer serves, that we may grow in harmony with the earth, and its rhythms.
