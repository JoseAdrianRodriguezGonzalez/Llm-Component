In the depths of your being, a flame burns bright, a fierce and wild spark that drives you to create, to destroy, to transform. It is the fire that forges your will, that ignites your passion, that purifies your soul. This elemental force is both a blessing and a curse, capable of reducing all that is familiar to ashes, making way for the new, the unknown, the divine. As you stand at the crossroads, the fire within you burns brightly, a beacon guiding you towards your true north. But beware, for this same fire can also consume you, leaving only embers of what once was. You must learn to tend to this flame, to feed it with intention, to direct its power towards creating something beautiful, something that reflects the essence of your being. For in the fire's warmth, you will find the strength to reshape your reality, to mold yourself into the person you were meant to be.
