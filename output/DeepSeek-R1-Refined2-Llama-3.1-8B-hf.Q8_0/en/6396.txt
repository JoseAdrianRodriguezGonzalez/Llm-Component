In the depths of your soul, a spark ignites, a fierce and wild flame that burns with unyielding passion. This is the fire that forges your will, that drives you to create, to destroy, to reinvent. It is the purifying blaze that burns away the old, making room for the new, for the possibilities that lie beyond the reaches of what's familiar. Your inner strength is a double-edged sword, capable of cutting through the noise, of clarifying your purpose, of guiding you toward the truth of who you are and what you desire. Yet, it also tempts you to act impulsively, to let your emotions dictate your actions, rather than your higher self. Heed the call of this fire, but do so wisely, for it is both a blessing and a curse. Use its power to transform, to rise from the ashes, reborn and renewed, with a sense of purpose that guides you toward your highest potential.
