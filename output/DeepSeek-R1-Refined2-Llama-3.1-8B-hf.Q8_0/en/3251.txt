In the stillness, three cups stand empty, their silence a reminder that sometimes, we must let go of the past to make room for the present. The fourth cup, full and overflowing, waits patiently, a symbol of the abundance that lies within reach. It is a time for reflection, for looking inward to understand the flow of life's resources, how they ebb and flow like the seasons. The earth beneath our feet, a steady foundation, reminds us that stability is key, yet it is also a realm where growth and transformation occur, where seeds are planted and nurtured. Amidst the stability, there is a sense of waiting, of patience, as the full cup anticipates the next chapter, filled with the promise of sustenance and joy.
