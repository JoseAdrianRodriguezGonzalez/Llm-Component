In the depths of the soul, a gentle lapping of waves against the shores of consciousness stirs the emotions, inviting exploration of the inner tides. The heart, a vessel for the ocean's depths, overflows with feelings that ebb and flow like the tides, carrying secrets and truths that only the darkness can reveal. Immersion in this inner world allows for the discovery of dominant currents and their impact on the surface of life, guiding the journey towards a deeper understanding of the heart's rhythms.
