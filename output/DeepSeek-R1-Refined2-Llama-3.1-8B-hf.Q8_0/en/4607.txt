In the depths of the soul, a spark glows like embers of a long-forgotten fire. This inner flame, a manifestation of the divine, pulses with the energy of creation and destruction. It burns away the remnants of what no longer serves, making way for the new to emerge, like a phoenix from the ashes. The queen, a guardian of this sacred fire, tends to it with compassion and wisdom, ensuring its light illuminates the path forward. Her presence is a reminder that the power to shape one's reality lies within, waiting to be fanned into a blaze of determination and willpower. As the flames dance, they reflect the turmoil and transformation that occurs when one chooses to embark on a journey of self-discovery and growth, guided by the innate wisdom of the heart.
