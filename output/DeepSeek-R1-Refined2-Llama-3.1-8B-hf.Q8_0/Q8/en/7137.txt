As the hermit tends the flame, it purifies the ashes of regret, forging a new path, where determination and courage entwine like the branches of a mighty tree. In this sanctuary of reflection, the seeker finds the strength to confront the shadows, to emerge transformed, with the light of knowledge illuminating the way forward.
