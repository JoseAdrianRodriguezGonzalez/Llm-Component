In the depths of the soul, a furnace burns, forging desires into action. The spark of creation ignites, casting shadows on the walls of the mind, where fears and doubts reside. Yet, from this same flame, courage is born, a beacon in the darkness, guiding the heart towards purpose. The fire of willpower rages, consuming what is no longer needed, making way for new beginnings. It is a purification, a cleansing of the old to reveal the truth within. The seeker is invited to tend this inner flame, to channel its energy wisely, that it may illuminate the path to transformation, and guide them towards a higher reality.
