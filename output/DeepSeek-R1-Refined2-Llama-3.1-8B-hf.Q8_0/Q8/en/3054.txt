In the depths of the soul, a spark ignites, a flame that flickers with the essence of creation. It is the fire that forges will, that burns away the ashes of what no longer serves, making room for the new to arise. This is the power that stirs within, the divine spark that sets the heart ablaze with passion and purpose. It is the driving force behind dreams, behind the courage to take the first step, to leap into the unknown with faith that the universe will support the journey. As the flames dance, they purify, casting away the old, the worn, the stagnant, making way for the rebirth of self, for the emergence of a being awakened, alive, and unafraid to burn brightly, to shine with the light of one's truest desires.
