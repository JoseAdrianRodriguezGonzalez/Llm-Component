In the depths of solitude, a flicker of inner flame dances, beckoning the soul to tend to its embers. This spark, a divine spark, ignites the passion that fuels the journey of self-discovery, guiding the wanderer through the labyrinth of their own heart. As the hermit tends the flame, they cultivate the will to navigate the shadows, where the old and the new converge. The fire crackles with the energy of creation, yet also whispers tales of destruction, reminding that which is stagnant must be purged to make way for the new. In this sacred space, the hermit finds the strength to reflect on their desires, to align their inner fire with the higher aspirations, and to wield their power consciously, forging a path of transformation and growth.
