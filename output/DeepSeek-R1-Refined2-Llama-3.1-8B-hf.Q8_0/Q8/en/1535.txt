The heart, once a raging sea, now lies still, its waves stilled by the weight of unexpressed emotions. In this frozen landscape, the seeker must embark on a journey of introspection, delving into the icy waters of their own emotions, to find the hidden currents that may yet lead them back to the surface, to rebirth and renewal.
