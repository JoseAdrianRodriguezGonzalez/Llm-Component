In the depths of your being, a spark ignites, fueled by the fires of passion and will. This inner flame burns brightly, guiding you towards action and change. It is the divine spark that sets your soul ablaze, motivating you to bring your deepest desires into the world. Yet, this fire is double-edged, capable of both creation and destruction. It purifies the old, making way for the new, but also warns of the dangers of unchecked emotions. To harness its power, you must reflect on your desires, questioning which ones align with your higher self. This card invites you to confront your inner strength, to acknowledge the drive that propels you, and to channel it towards constructive ends. Only then can you truly transform, emerging from the ashes of your old self, reborn into a brighter, more authentic you.
