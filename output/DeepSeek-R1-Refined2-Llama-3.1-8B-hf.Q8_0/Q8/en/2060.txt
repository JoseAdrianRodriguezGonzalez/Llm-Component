In the depths of the soul, a knight emerges, borne on the currents of the heart, where feelings flow like the tides. He carries a cup, a chalice that overflows with the nectar of emotions, a symbol of the subconscious mind. As he navigates the waters of the psyche, he encounters the hidden recesses of the heart, where desires and fears entwine like sea serpents. With each step, he collects the tears of sorrow and the laughter of joy, storing them in his cup, a vessel that reflects the ever-changing landscape of the emotional world. His presence invites the seeker to dive into the abyss, to explore the depths of their own feelings, and to emerge transformed, with a deeper understanding of the tides that shape their life.
