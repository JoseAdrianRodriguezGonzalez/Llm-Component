In the depths of your being, a fierce and radiant flame burns, a spark of the divine that ignites your passions and fuels your will to shape the world around you. This inner fire is both a creative force, birthing new ideas and possibilities, and a purifying blaze, consuming the old and making way for transformation. It is the engine of your determination, driving you to take bold action and push beyond the boundaries of what is possible. Yet, this power is double-edged, capable of destroying as easily as it creates. To harness its energy, you must acknowledge and respect its force, using it to align your desires with a higher purpose, and to reinvent yourself in the image of your authentic dreams.
