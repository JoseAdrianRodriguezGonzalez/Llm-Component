In the stillness of decay, new life stirs, like a seed germinating in the darkness of winter's snow. The cycle of transformation unfolds, a dance of death and rebirth, where the old yields to the new, surrendering its form to make way for the unbridled potential of growth. The earth, a crucible of creation, churns and transforms, recycling the past to forge the future, a relentless alchemist turns lead into gold, transmuting the base into the sublime. In this realm, change is not endings, but beginnings, a vortex of energy that swirls, collects, and releases, reminding us that stability is but an illusion, a momentary pause between the eternal flux of becoming.
