In the depths of your being, a flame burns bright, a fierce and wild spark that drives you to create, to destroy, to reinvent. This fire within you is both blessing and curse, a double-edged sword that cuts away the old to make room for the new. It is the engine of your passion, the fuel for your dreams, and the heat that forges your will into steel. But beware, for this same fire can consume you, leaving ashes and regret in its wake. To harness its power, you must learn to tend the flames, to feed them with purpose and intention, lest they rage out of control, burning everything in their path. The choice is yours: to let the fire guide you towards transformation, or to be consumed by its unbridled energy. The path ahead is fraught with danger, yet also holds the promise of rebirth and renewal. Will you dance with the flames, or will you let them reduce you to embers? The decision is yours, and yours alone.
