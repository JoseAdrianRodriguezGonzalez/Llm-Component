In the stillness of the silent lake, two swans glide effortlessly, their reflections rippling like whispers on the water's surface. One swan represents the realm of thoughts, where ideas birthed in the mind's eye take flight, while the other embodies the domain of emotions, where heart's desires ebb and flow like the tides. As they balance, poised on the water's edge, they symbolize the delicate dance between intuition and reason, where the subtle and the obvious converge. In this mirrored symmetry, we find the harmony that arises when our inner worlds are in balance, reflecting the stability and peace that can be ours when we listen to the whispers of our soul and the wisdom of the physical world.
