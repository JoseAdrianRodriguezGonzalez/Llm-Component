In the depths of your soul, a spark burns bright, a fierce and unyielding flame that fuels your every endeavor. This inner fire, a manifestation of the divine, courses through your veins, driving you to create, to inspire, and to bring your visions to life. Yet, beware, for this same fire can also consume you, reducing all that is old and worn to ashes, making way for the new, but also risking chaos and destruction. You must learn to tend this flame, to channel its energy wisely, that it may guide you towards your highest aspirations, rather than leading you down a path of self-destruction. The power is within you, wait for it to rise, and let it illuminate your journey, forging you into the strongest version of yourself.
