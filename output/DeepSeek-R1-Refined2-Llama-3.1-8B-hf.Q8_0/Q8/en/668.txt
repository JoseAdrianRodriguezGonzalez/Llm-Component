In this serene and introspective space, the stillness of the mind is reflected in the quiet solitude of a peaceful refuge. The walls that once confined and protected now stand as a testament to the need for retreat and rejuvenation. The four figures, emblematic of the four cardinal directions, lie resting, their faces serene, their bodies relaxed, surrounded by the solemnity of four swords, points downward, symbolizing the end of struggle and the beginning of healing. This scene represents the culmination of a journey, where the turbulence of life has subsided, and the calm of reflection has been achieved. The earth below, stable and unshakeable, provides a foundation for this inner peace, reminding us that serenity is not just a state of mind but also a reflection of our connection to the natural world. In this tranquil atmosphere, the boundaries between the internal and external worlds blur, inviting us to explore the harmony that can be found when spirit and matter are in balance.
