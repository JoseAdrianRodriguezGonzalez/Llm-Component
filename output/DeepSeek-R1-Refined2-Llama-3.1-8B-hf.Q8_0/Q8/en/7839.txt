In the depths of the soul, a fierce spark burns, a flame that flickers with the passion of creation and the fury of destruction. It is the fire that forges willpower, that ignites the drive to shape reality and to burn away the ashes of what no longer serves. This inner fire is both a blessing and a curse, a double-edged sword that cuts through the veil of doubt and fear, revealing the deepest desires and the courage to act. It is the spark that sets the heart ablaze, that fuels the imagination and inspires the vision to take form. Yet, it is also the flame that consumes the old, making way for the new, purification through fire, a transformative power that reshapes the self and the world.
