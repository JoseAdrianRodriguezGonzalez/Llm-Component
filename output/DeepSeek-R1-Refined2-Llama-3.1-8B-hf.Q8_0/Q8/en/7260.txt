In the depths of the soul, a great transformation unfolds, like the tides that ebb and flow, revealing the hidden treasures of the inner world. The boundaries that once separated the conscious from the subconscious begin to blur, allowing the depths of emotion to rise to the surface. Like a river overflowing its banks, the feelings that were once contained now flow freely, washing away the dust of forgetfulness and revealing the secrets that were hidden in the depths. This is a time of rebirth, where the old must die to make way for the new, and the emotional landscape is rewritten. The waves of feeling crash against the shores of the mind, reminding us that emotional sensitivity is a gift, a tool for connection with others and with our own hearts. As we navigate this turbulent yet beautiful landscape, we are offered the chance to rediscover ourselves, to understand the depths of our own emotions, and to emerge transformed, like the lotus that rises from the mud, unsoiled and renewed.
