In the depths of your being, a spark ignites, a flame that flickers with the essence of creation. It is the fire that forges will, that shapes desire into action, and that purifies the old to make way for the new. As you navigate the landscape of your soul, recognize the energy that drives you, the passion that fuels your endeavors.
