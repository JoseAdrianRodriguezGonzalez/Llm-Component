The darkness is not to be feared, but rather, it is a gateway to the unseen, a realm where intuition and feeling converge. As the Waters of the subconscious rise and fall, they carry the secrets of the self, revealing hidden truths to those who dare to dive into the depths. In this world of reflection, the seeker finds the keys to unlocking the mysteries of the emotions, and with each breath, they are drawn deeper into the abyss, where the light of knowledge awaits.
