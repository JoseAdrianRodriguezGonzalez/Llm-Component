As the embers of longing fade, like tears dried on worn velvet, a subtle shift stirs within the depths of the soul. The flame that once burned brightly, a beacon of passion and will, now simmers, its warmth still palpable, yet its radiance diminished. This is not a surrender, but a transformation, for in the stillness, a new spark awaits, like a promise whispered in the darkness. The ashes of what once was, though buried, hold the secrets of a rebirth, a purification by fire, where the old is consumed, making room for the unknown. In this pause, the heart must reflect on the desires that drove the past, and consider which embers to tend, and which to let fade, that the new path, forged in the fire of self-discovery, may lead to a destiny ignited by the divine spark within.
