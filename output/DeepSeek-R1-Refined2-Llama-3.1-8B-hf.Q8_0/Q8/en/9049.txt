In the depths of the soul, a slow exodus unfolds, as one by one, the cups that once held the heart's desires are left behind, empty and still. The tide of emotions, once a raging sea, has begun to ebb, revealing the sandy shoreline of forgotten longings. The mirror of the subconscious reflects a landscape both familiar and strange, where the shadows of past loves and losses linger, like ripples on the water's surface. As the heart moves on, it carries the weight of what has been left behind, a poignant reminder of the transience of desire and the certainty of change. In this melancholic yet liberating space, the seeker is invited to dive into the depths of their own emotional ocean, to explore the hidden caves of their heart, and to emerge with a newfound appreciation for the ebb and flow of life.
