In the depths of the soul, a spark glows like embers of a long-dormant fire. This inner flame, a manifestation of the divine, pulses with the energy of creation and destruction. It burns away the ashes of what once was, making room for the new to rise from the ashes. The drive to act, to will, to manifest one's desires is fueled by this spark, a reminder that the power to shape reality resides within. Yet, this fire also purifies, burning away the impurities of doubt and fear, inviting reflection on the desires that drive us. As we tend this inner flame, we must align our actions with the higher purpose it illuminates, using our willpower consciously to foster growth and transformation, becoming the architects of our own destiny.
