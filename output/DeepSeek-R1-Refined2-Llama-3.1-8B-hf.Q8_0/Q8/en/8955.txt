In the depths of the soul, a spark ignites, a flame that flickers with the essence of creation. It is the fire that burns within, a passionate cry for expression, a will to shape the world according to one's deepest desires. This inner turmoil, a maelstrom of emotions, is both destructive and redeeming, burning away the old to make room for the new. It is the purifying force that cleanses the heart, clarifying the vision, and focusing the intent. As the fire rages, it illuminates the path, revealing the secrets of the subconscious, and guiding the individual toward self-discovery. With each wave of heat, the barriers dissolve, allowing the true self to emerge, unencumbered by the weights of doubt and fear. The fire card is a call to acknowledge this inner power, to harness it, and to channel it toward a higher purpose, where creativity and inspiration knows no bounds. It is an invitation to embark on a journey of self-reinvention, where the ashes of the old self become the fertile ground for a new, reborn entity to rise.
