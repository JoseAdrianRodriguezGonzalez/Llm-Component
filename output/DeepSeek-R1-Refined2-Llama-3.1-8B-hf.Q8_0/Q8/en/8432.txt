In the stillness of darkness, a great transformation unfolds, like a seed buried beneath the earth, waiting for the spring's warmth to awaken its potential. The cycles of nature reveal the truth that endings mark beginnings, and what seems like demise is actually rebirth. The physical realm, once a source of comfort and security, now presents challenges that test one's resolve. Resources, once abundant, may seem scarce, but this is an opportunity to reassess one's connection to the natural world and the body that inhabits it. The time has come to let go of what no longer serves, making space for the new to emerge, like a lotus flower pushing through the muddy waters, unencumbered by the weight of the past. This is a time of surrender, of allowing the old to crumble, making way for the fertile soil of new possibilities to nourish the soul.
