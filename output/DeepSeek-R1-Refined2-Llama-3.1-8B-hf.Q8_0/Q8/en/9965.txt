The air is thick with the perfume of possibility, yet you are unable to breathe deeply, for your lungs are starved of the oxygen of clarity. You must learn to still the winds of doubt, to calm the tempests of fear, and to listen to the silent voice within, for it is there that the secrets of the universe await your attention.
