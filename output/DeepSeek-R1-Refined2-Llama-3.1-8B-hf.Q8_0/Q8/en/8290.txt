In the depths of the soul, a spark glows like embers, waiting to be fanned into flames. This inner fire burns with an insatiable hunger, driving the seeker to quest for meaning and purpose. It is the spark of creation, the divine spark that ignites the passion to bring ideas into being. Yet, this fire also ravages, burning away the old to make way for the new, purifying the soul through transformation. The seeker must tend this flame, nurturing it with reflection and intention, that it may guide them towards their highest aspirations. As they gaze into its depths, they will discover the secrets of their own willpower, and learn to harness it wisely, lest it consume them in its fierce blaze. The fire crackles with energy, a potent force that can shape reality, if only they dare to unleash its full potential.
