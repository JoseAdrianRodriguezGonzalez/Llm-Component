In the depths of the soul, a monarch reigns, wielding the power of discernment and reason. His dominion is one of clarity, where the murkiness of emotions is clarified, and the subconscious is brought to the surface. Like a still pond, he reflects the hidden truths, allowing the individual to gaze upon their innermost feelings. This king's wisdom is not one of passion, but of calm observation, where the ebb and flow of emotions are understood as a natural phenomenon. His presence invites the seeker to embark on an inward journey, to navigate the currents of their emotional landscape, and to find peace in the midst of turmoil. Through his leadership, the boundaries between the self and the world are blurred, facilitating a deeper connection with others and a more authentic understanding of one's own heart.
