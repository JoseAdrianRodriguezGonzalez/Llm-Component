In this sacred marriage of spirit and matter, we find the power to nurture and sustain, to cultivate and harvest, our deepest desires and values. The wheel turns, and with it, the opportunity to re-align our practices with the essence of our being, to embody the wisdom of the earth, and to walk the path of balance and fulfillment.
