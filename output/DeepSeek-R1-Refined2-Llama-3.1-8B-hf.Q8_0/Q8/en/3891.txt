Shadows dance upon the walls, whispers of forgotten fears and doubts that linger, refusing to be silenced. The heart, a buoy, is tossed about, helpless against the tides of emotion. Tears fall like rain, a deluge of sorrow that threatens to overwhelm. And yet, in this turmoil, a spark of wisdom glows, a lantern that guides through the storm. It is a reminder that the depths are treacherous, but also where the greatest treasures lie, hidden beneath the surface, waiting to be uncovered.
